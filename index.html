Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering easy meals for university students.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You can order whenever it suits you, with no recurring commitment.

How many meals can I order?

You can order up to three meals per pickup day across any of the available meal kits.

Where do I pick up my meals?

Pickup is offered from a fictional campus-area location in Antigonish near StFX. The exact location is listed during checkout.

How far in advance should I order?

Please order at least two days before your selected pickup date.

What is included in each meal kit?

Each kit includes pre-portioned ingredients and a step-by-step recipe card for one serving.

What cooking equipment do I need?

Most meals need basic equipment such as a saucepan, frying pan, knife and cutting board.

Are vegetarian options available?

Yes. The Southwest Black Bean Bowl and Tomato and Basil Pesto Pasta are vegetarian.

What happens if a meal is sold out?

Unavailable meals cannot be added to the cart. We recommend another meal from the menu.

How do I know if my order is confirmed?

After successful demonstration checkout, you receive an on-screen and simulated email confirmation.
